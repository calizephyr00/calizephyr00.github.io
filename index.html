Home Control

A personal Mac menu bar app for one household's light switches and Nest thermostat.

Home Control puts the household's light switches and thermostat in a small panel in the Mac menu bar, so they can be checked and adjusted without picking up a phone.

What it does

  • Shows each light switch as on or off and turns it on or off.
  • Shows the thermostat's mode, target temperature and indoor temperature, and changes the target and mode.

Who it's for

Home Control is a private, single-household tool. It is not sold, distributed or offered to the public, and it runs only on its owner's own Mac.

Google Nest access

The thermostat is reached through Google's Smart Device Management API, with the owner's own permission granted through Google's Nest consent screen. See the privacy policy for exactly what that covers.
